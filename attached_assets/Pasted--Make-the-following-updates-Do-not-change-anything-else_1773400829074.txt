CAREGIVER SELF NEGLECT:
What you are describing about yourself is important and needs attention.
Caregivers are at high risk of neglecting their own health — skipping meals, losing sleep, postponing their own medical care, and ignoring symptoms they would never ignore in their loved one. This is extremely common and extremely serious.
What self neglect looks like:
	∙	Not eating regularly or eating poorly
	∙	Chronic sleep deprivation
	∙	Postponing or canceling your own doctor appointments
	∙	Not taking your own medications consistently
	∙	Ignoring physical symptoms — pain, fatigue, changes in your own health
	∙	Withdrawing from friends and activities you used to enjoy
	∙	Feeling like your needs do not matter compared to your loved one’s

If your health fails the person you are caring for loses their caregiver. Your health is not separate from their care — it is part of it.
